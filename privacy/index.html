Shadomo Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Shadomo runs entirely on your iPhone. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data to us or to any third party.

Camera

  • On-device only. Shadomo uses Apple's Vision framework to read body-pose points from the camera in order to count moves. Frames are processed in memory and discarded immediately.
  • No video is stored or sent. Nothing from the camera is saved to your phone or uploaded anywhere.
  • No face recognition. Shadomo detects body joints, never faces or identity.
  • Share cards you choose to create contain only a stick-figure skeleton drawn from those joints, never camera pixels.

Screen Time

  • Shadomo uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) APIs only to shield the apps you pick for yourself, and to lift the shield after a move.
  • Shadomo cannot see which apps you use or for how long. App identities stay inside Apple's system and never reach us.
  • Shadomo is not a parental-control product and involves no child accounts.

Your records

  • Moves done, minutes earned, streaks and settings are stored locally in the app. You can delete everything from Settings → Privacy & data.
  • Widgets read a small summary (minutes today, streak, next move) shared only with the app's own extensions on your phone.

Purchases

Shadomo Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment information.

Accounts, analytics, ads

No sign-up, no login. The app contains no advertising SDKs. If we add anonymous, aggregate usage statistics in a future version, they will contain no identifiers and this page will say so before it takes effect.
