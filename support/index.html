Shadomo Support

Counting misses my squats

Lean the phone on a cup or a book at about 2 metres, step back until your hips, knees and ankles are inside the outline, and turn on a light. The rear camera sees wider if you cannot get far enough back (Settings → Gate → Camera).

An app did not lock again

Open Shadomo once; it re-locks anything whose time ran out while it was closed. If Screen Time access was turned off, turn it back on in iOS Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time access.

Restore my subscription on a new phone

Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Delete my data

Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all local data. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
