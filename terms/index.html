What Shadomo is

  • Shadomo is a habit tool: you choose apps to gate and open them by doing a short physical move. It is not medical advice and not a fitness or health assessment. Stop any move that hurts, and check with a professional before exercising if you have a health condition.
  • You set up every gate for yourself. Shadomo is not intended for controlling another person's device.

Shadomo Pro

  • Shadomo Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (yearly or monthly) sold through the App Store. The yearly plan may include a free trial; you will not be charged until the trial ends.

Fair use

Move detection is done by software on your phone and can miss or miscount. The app offers a manual pass when detection fails; using it repeatedly makes the next unlock cost more by design.
